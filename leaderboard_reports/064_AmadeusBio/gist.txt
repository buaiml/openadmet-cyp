# OpenADMET CYP Inhibition Blind Challenge — Method Report

Entry submitted under the Hugging Face account used on the submission form,
displayed on the leaderboard under aliases (`AmadeusBio` for the regression and
structure tracks, `CYP Research` for the historical classification entry).
Author identity and affiliation are deliberately withheld; the aliases are a
display name, not an author list. No proprietary training data was used. Code
and model weights are not released, so the "open-source code" box is not
checked.

This report covers all three tracks the entry participates in. It describes the
submissions as accepted, including the limitations and the selection exposure
each one carries.

---

## 1. Direct inhibition (regression) — four pIC50 heads

**Predictor.** For CYP1A2, CYP2C9 and CYP3A4, a fixed equal-weight average of a
robust linear model and the mean of three auxiliary-supervised neural networks.
For CYP2D6, that same base prediction passed through a monotone placement map
fitted by a censored likelihood. Four continuous pIC50 values per test compound.

**Supervision and separation.** Organizer-released direct-inhibition
measurements plus the released single-concentration `log2fc_estimate` screen for
the same four isoforms as an auxiliary target. The permitted development pool is
3,913 compounds in 3,627 structure components, with 1,105 / 1,040 / 1,167 /
1,888 observed primary labels in the order above. Missing endpoint values are
masked, never imputed. Reserved compounds and their whole connected structure
groups (1,229 compounds) are excluded before every join, label read, fitted
statistic and learned transform. Test-time input is compound identifier and
SMILES only; the identifier is used for row alignment and is not a feature.

**Features.** 2,048-bit radius-2 Morgan fingerprint plus the full ordered
217-descriptor RDKit list. Descriptors are cast to float32 and clipped to
±1e6. TruncatedSVD (256 components, `random_state=0`) is fitted on gradient
rows only, concatenated with the 217 descriptors, and standardized by a
gradient-fitted scaler, giving 473 inputs. The saved projection and scaling are
reused at inference; stopping and test rows never fit a transform.

**Neural component.** A shared MLP, 473→512→256, BatchNorm + ReLU + dropout 0.25
after each hidden layer, with four primary and four auxiliary heads of
256→64→1. Each endpoint is standardized by the mean and population standard
deviation of its observed gradient labels. The objective is the mean squared
residual over observed standardized primary entries plus 0.3× the same over
observed auxiliary entries; missing entries contribute nothing. AdamW, lr 1e-3,
weight decay 1e-5, batch 256, cosine annealing with `T_max=300`, at most 300
epochs, patience 40 on primary-only observed-entry MSE, best state restored, no
post-stopping refit. Three deployment fits (seeds 0/1/2) ran 73/112/116 epochs.
Each seed holds out its own 20% component-disjoint stopping partition.

**Robust linear component.** 2,048 radius-2 Morgan *counts* plus ten descriptors
(MW, logP, TPSA, HBD, HBA, rotatable bonds, total and aromatic rings, Fsp3,
heavy atoms), raw and unscaled, float64, unpenalized intercept. For a signed
residual `r` outside the released lower/upper confidence band and `r = 0` inside
it, the loss is `2δ²(sqrt(1 + (r/δ)²) − 1) + α‖w‖²` with `α = 100`, `δ = 0.5`.
Ridge initialization then L-BFGS-B. The released uncertainty bands are used as a
dead zone in this loss; we do not assert they are censoring limits.

**CYP2D6 placement correction.** The released assay reports a fitted
dose-response curve only for compounds the single-concentration screen selected.
Treating the unselected rows as missing at random places the CYP2D6 head at the
location of the hit-selected subpopulation rather than that of the test deck.
With `c = 4.30535` (the negative log10 of the single tested screen
concentration), we fit a monotone piecewise-linear map `g(s)` with five knots at
the 0/25/50/75/100 percentiles of the base index, maximizing
`Normal(y; g(s), τ² + σᵢ²)` on curve-fitted rows using the released per-row
standard deviation, plus `log Φ((c − g(s))/τ)` on screened-but-unfitted rows.
L-BFGS-B, `maxiter` 2000, tol 1e-10, seeds 0/1/2. The index is the base model's
retained out-of-fold prediction in development and its submitted blinded
prediction at inference; the base model is not refitted. `g` is fitted once on
all permitted CYP2D6 development rows (1,167 fitted, 2,300 censored).

The map is applied to **one head only**, under a gate stated before fitting: a
head is admitted only where the likelihood's implication "unfitted implies
y ≤ c" is essentially deterministic, measured as the rank AUC of the released
screen's own selection against potency, threshold 0.99. Where selection has rank
AUC `a`, a fraction of order `(1 − a)` of unfitted rows violate the implication,
the likelihood is misspecified, and the estimate is biased downward because it
forces genuinely active mass below the threshold. Measured selection AUC is
0.8961 (CYP1A2), 0.5958 (CYP2C9), 0.9998 (CYP2D6), 0.4114 (CYP3A4), so CYP2D6
alone is admitted. An earlier iteration at a 0.85 threshold over-corrected
CYP1A2 exactly as this predicts. On the 750 submitted CYP2D6 values the realized
effect is mean 4.6659 → 3.8563 (shift -0.8096) and standard deviation 0.3604 →
0.7772; because `g` is monotone the within-head ordering of all 750 compounds is
unchanged, asserted numerically before export along with byte-identity of the
other three heads.

**What was deliberately not applied.** The exact pointwise Bayes act for the
soft-thresholded relative absolute error is derived and unit-tested — the loss
has a dead zone inside the released band and its Bayes act solves
`F_U(t) + F_L(t) = 1`, an interval median collapsing to the conditional median
for a symmetric band. Its empirical gates failed twice: the pre-registered
decision arm moved the fitted-row macro from 0.7196 to 0.7311 (worse by 0.0115)
while an unrestricted arm reached 1.0275, breaching a 0.15 guardrail, and a
later mixture form reached 1.2084. Only the placement correction ships.

**Validation, and an honest limit.** The base recipe was nominated in an exposed
five-fold grouped development study (macro ST-RAE 0.6951 against 0.7202 for its
incumbent reference); that figure describes the out-of-fold procedure, not
cross-validation of the three final fitted networks, and fixed-OOF intervals
omit refitting and selection uncertainty. The placement correction has **no
valid local falsifier**: released labels exist only in the hit-selected region,
inverse-probability weighting fails positivity below the screen threshold, and
the released Emax arm has identical coverage to pIC50. A direction and a
magnitude interval with an explicit falsification condition were therefore
recorded before upload. The official board was the only available test. The
official validation script passed on the exported CSV and parity against the
retained reference held to 1e-12.

**Selection exposure — stated plainly.** The 0.99 gate threshold was tightened
*after* this work had read our own official per-head feedback for the superseded
submission and solved per-head offsets from it. It is therefore **not a blind
pre-registration** and should not be read as one. Two prior failed iterations in
the same family add further selection exposure. No official-feedback quantity,
no leaderboard-derived moment and no third-party reconstruction of a blind mean,
standard deviation or prevalence enters any fitted value, knot, scale or shift;
the correction magnitude is whatever the released-data censored likelihood
returns. The pre-registered direction was confirmed on the board and the
pre-registered *magnitude* was not — the realized values fell below both stated
intervals, which we record as a missed magnitude prediction rather than a
success. The estimator is consistent for the screened composition, a condition
not checkable from released data, and a separate measurement found it does not
transfer to the other three heads.

## 2. Time-dependent inhibition (classification) — CYP2D6 and CYP3A4

**Predictor.** `TDI-BALANCED-TREES-001`: the mean of five previously retained
grouped-fold ExtraTrees forests per endpoint, thresholded at two previously
selected cutoffs. No new all-development model was fitted for deployment.

**Data and grouping.** Released Boolean `CYP2D6_is_TDI` and `CYP3A4_is_TDI`
labels. The original procedure held 6,145 source structures: 4,916 development
and 1,229 reserved and excluded, with 1,171 observed CYP2D6 and 2,899 observed
CYP3A4 labels. Each forest used only observed labels outside its held-out fold.
Missing values were not imputed, and **continuous shift measurements were never
converted into replacement Boolean truth**. Grouping used non-generic
Bemis–Murcko scaffolds: reserve fold 0 of 5 at seed 20261004, then five
development folds recomputed at seed 0. A later whole-component connectivity
protection produced a different 4,912-row universe; the submitted forests retain
their original 4,916-row boundary, and we do not attribute the later protection
or its nested estimates to them.

**Features and estimator.** Identifier and SMILES only at inference. A 2,048-bin
radius-2 Morgan count fingerprint concatenated with the same ten descriptors
listed above, giving 2,058 raw float32 features with no fitted scaling, PCA,
graph encoder, pretrained embedding or probability calibration. Each
endpoint/fold model is an `ExtraTreesClassifier` with 256 trees, Gini splitting,
`max_features=0.5`, `min_samples_leaf=3`, `min_samples_split=2`, unlimited
depth, `bootstrap=False`, `random_state=0`, `class_weight="balanced"`. Class
weights are computed within each forest's own observed TRAIN labels; no extra
sample-weight multiplier or balanced bootstrap sampler is used.

**Cutoffs and inference.** Five-fold development out-of-fold probabilities chose
one cutoff per endpoint by maximum MCC over 91 values from 0.05 to 0.95,
strict-improvement tie-breaking to the lowest cutoff: **0.30 for CYP2D6 and 0.33
for CYP3A4**. These are global development-OOF choices, not separately nested
inside outer TRAIN contexts. At inference, ten saved forests are loaded, the
positive class is located via `model.classes_`, the five positive-class
probabilities are averaged per endpoint, and the submitted Boolean is
`probability >= cutoff`. No fit, recalibration or cutoff search happens at
deployment. Probabilities were retained; the CSV carries Boolean calls.

**Validation limits.** The original exposed grouped screen reported macro MCC
0.2846 under a 200-bootstrap global-OOF-cutoff procedure. That is not the score
of a new all-development refit, and it differs from a later protected nested
reference of 0.2509 whose grouping, label counts, resampling and
threshold-selection all changed. Each historical OOF row is predicted by its
excluded-fold forest, whereas each test row is predicted by the mean of all
five. Model-family selection, the global OOF threshold choice and subsequent
public feedback are all exposed; historical conditional row-bootstrap intervals
omit model and threshold selection and scaffold dependence. Reserved and blinded
evaluation are unrun, and the registry discloses historical reserved-arithmetic
access, which excluding reserved labels from these fits does not erase.

## 3. CYP3A4 structure prediction — attributed provided baseline, with one validity repair

**This is not our own structure model, and we make no claim that it is.** We did
not train or run a co-folding model, perform docking, optimize an energy
function or rank newly generated poses.

**Source.** The organizer's publicly provided sample Boltz-2 predictions:
`OpenADMET/CYP-Challenge-Tutorial` at commit
`832ae6d6447b07c2c71059de34d07f1130388247`, directory
`inputs/boltz-2_cyp3a4_predictions/`, one supplied `model_0` CIF per required
complex. The pinned tutorial notebook identifies these as sample Boltz-2
predictions and demonstrates their conversion. This attribution does not imply
authorship of Boltz-2 or reproduction of the organizer's generation process; the
underlying checkpoint, its training corpus and its generation settings are not
established by us and we do not guess at them.

**Conversion.** A converter reads the declared single-model atom-site layout,
checks the expected protein, heme and ligand namespaces, and writes each complex
as a PDB retaining the provided coordinates at the declared precision
(coordinates to three decimals, occupancy and B-factor to two). The ligand
residue `LIG1` is renamed `LIG`, serials are assigned, required filenames are
written, and the 20 PDBs are packaged as one ZIP. No relaxation, conformer
generation, hydrogen reconstruction or geometry-based rescue is involved. No
learned feature, loss, fitted parameter, train/validation split or stopping rule
is introduced by the conversion, and no blinded reference complex or assay
outcome was used to choose or optimize coordinates.

**The one change — a validity-gate repair.** The scoring backend applies
PoseBusters with a zero-failure policy, so a single failed check overrides that
pose's LDDT-PLI to 0 before best-pair selection. Of the 20 provided poses,
exactly one (`OCNT-2313429`) fell below the `minimum_distance_to_protein`
relative-distance floor, at 0.7257 against the 0.75 threshold, and was therefore
contributing exactly zero. We applied a **rigid-body transform (3 translation,
3 rotation) to the ligand chain only**, moving it by 0.173 Å ligand heavy-atom
RMSD — enough to clear the floor. A rigid transform preserves bond lengths,
angles and planarity by construction. The other **19 members are byte-identical**
to the previously accepted submission, so their scores cannot change. The
repaired file differs on 23 of 4,910 lines, none outside the coordinate fields.
Gate verification used PoseBusters 0.6.5 with `config='dock'`, 22 boolean
checks: the original failure `minimum_distance_to_protein` is cleared and no
check fails after the repair.

We deliberately did **not** move the three next-closest poses (relative
distances 0.7581, 0.7721, 0.7746). They pass the full battery, so moving them
has no mechanism for gain and would perturb poses that currently score. The
expectation recorded before dispatch was "non-negative, magnitude not locally
predictable", because the true LDDT-PLI of the repaired complex is not
observable locally. Clearing a validity gate is not evidence of physical
adequacy, and physical validity and LDDT-PLI are different outcomes.

## Attribution and tooling

The neural recipe was adapted from `Rebell-Leader/OpenADMET_CYP` at commit
`76143594ed320a1324ff0146d798163bdd209c3d` (`run_multitask.py`,
`build_features.py`, `multitask.py`). Our implementation fits transforms only on
its own gradient partition and keeps its own serialization boundaries; it is not
a byte-identical reproduction of that repository's results. The provided pose
assets come from the Apache-2.0 licensed challenge tutorial repository, with
source and change notices plus a copy of the upstream license in every PDB's
REMARK records; the repository-license application to the provided assets is our
interpretation, and complete rights ancestry is not certified here.

Tooling: RDKit, scikit-learn, SciPy, PyTorch, NumPy, pandas, PoseBusters 0.6.5.
Scores quoted in this report are public leaderboard figures for the submissions
as accepted. They are not estimates of hidden-test performance, and no claim of
final rank, leader parity or state of the art is made or implied anywhere in
this report.
