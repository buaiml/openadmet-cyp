Multitask graph networks drive
the activity predictions

Twenty original fold fits

Randomly initialized Chemprop 2.3 directed message-passing networks cover four training scopes, each with five scaffold-group folds and seeds 42–46.

The scopes contain a 26-target public/challenge union, eight challenge tasks, six D6 isoform tasks, and single-target D6.

Adam uses batch 64. A two-epoch warmup raises the learning rate from 10−4 to 10−3, followed by decay toward 10−4. Maximum 400 epochs, patience 40, best validation checkpoint.

foundation-model hybrid

The branches use seven original seeds. Neural fine-tuning uses AdamW, encoder/head learning rates 10−4/3×10−4, batch 128, two 1,024-unit ReLU stages and dropout 0.1. An internal 15% scaffold holdout selects an epoch within 50 epochs, patience 8, followed by refitting.

Both source separation and ensemble composition matter. Historical tuning used a 0.1 mixture-weight grid and four disagreement-correction values.

TDI combines fingerprint trees
with a pretrained tabular reader

D6: five ExtraTrees classifiers

1,497 finite released labels, radius-2 Morgan counts and 2,048 bins. Each model uses 600 trees, balanced classes, Gini impurity, square-root feature sampling, unlimited depth and no bootstrap.

Seeds: 42, 7, 2024, 200, 201. Mean probabilities determine a rank, with molecule-name tie-breaking. The fixed policy calls the highest 53 of 750 positive.

A4: LightGBM plus TabICL

LightGBM uses ECFP4/RDKit2D features, 500 rounds, learning rate 0.05, 31 leaves, column/constructor subsample 0.8, balanced classes and seed 42.

The TabICL branch uses a frozen public 600D molecular encoder, training-only median imputation, scaling and PCA200. Eight estimators, seed 42.

Logistic features: [p0, p1, p0·p1]

The A4 combiner uses training-only out-of-fold probabilities, balanced L2 logistic regression, C = 1, an intercept, lbfgs and cutoff 0.5. Six fixed combiner fits cover five canonical-excluding supports and the full-training OOF support. This interaction stage adds no base-model fits or hyperparameter optimization (HPO).

A4 has 331 positive calls. The fixed D6 call count is an operating policy, not an estimate of hidden prevalence.6 / 15

The evidence supports an agent
workflow, with important limits

Selection and generalization

Historical scope, blend, calibration and operating-policy searches used internal and public feedback. HPO counts differ by stage and remain incomplete across the campaign.

Later fixed recipes often used zero predictive HPO. Some still selected epochs or training-only calibration maps. “HPO 0” does not remove every form of selection.

Scaffold/analogue contexts overlap and were repeatedly exposed. Public responses describe whole policies on the public subset. They do not establish private-test performance or isolated architecture effects.

Execution and attribution

Missed submission opportunities and idle-VM accumulation required human correction. Reuse and verified cleanup became explicit operating requirements.

Some early activity fits retain predictions/reports rather than every saved model state. Complete legacy replay remains unresolved. Pretraining membership relies partly on authors’ checkpoint-specific descriptions.

Changing LLMs, methods and compute over time prevents a causal ranking of LLM quality. Active LLM hours, human hours and campaign-wide costs were not fully audited.

The campaign demonstrates broad agent-executed experimentation with human steering. It also documents substantial negative transfer and operational failure.

Some historical routes remain outside the submitted methods because data provenance is unresolved. Structural prediction is outside this report.14 / 15
